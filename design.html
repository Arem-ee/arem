Design

Six tactile studies connected in continuous kinetic gravity. Trays, basins, and falling fluid motion.

Study One

Kinetic tension balance and dynamic fluid containment within tactile stoneware vessels.

Study Two

Refractive boundary conditions and specular reflections across cascading plane steps.

Study Three

Viscous momentum damping calibrated to real-time physical gravity and surface tension.

Study Four

Study Five

Fluid droplet dispersion and micro-droplet detachment curves under subtle air drafts.

Study Six

Final trajectory stabilization spilling directly into the collection pool basin below.
